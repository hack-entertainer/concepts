## The Process Pipeline

### Stage 1 — Filtration
Simple physical filtration of solid particulates from used fry oil. Solved. Off the shelf. Move on.

### Stage 2 — Drying (Two Phase)
A sequential, single-operation-at-a-time approach. No conglomerated fractions. Clean handoffs.

- **Phase 2a — Low Pressure Evaporation**
  - 5–10 mbar, 60–70°C maximum
  - Water evacuates first, followed by light volatile aldehydes and ketones (the off-smell compounds)
  - Nothing introduced. Pure physics.
  - Industrial implementation: thin film evaporator (rotary evap principle at scale)
  - Condensate captured in cold trap — not discarded. Analyzed. (See: Chicken Drips™)

- **Phase 2b — Molecular Sieve Drying**
  - 3 Angstrom sieves capture residual water molecules evaporation missed
  - Sieves regenerable and reusable
  - Inline water activity meter confirms completion before material advances

### Stage 3 — Steam Stripping & Fractional Distillation
Steam and oil run countercurrent through a distillation column.

**Column output fractions (top to bottom):**
- **Top** — water, light volatiles, short chain fatty acids → discard or analyze
- **Middle** — free fatty acids → recoverable separate stream, industrial food uses
- **Bottom** — polymerized and oxidized compounds → **fuel fraction**

**Key insight:** Neutral triglycerides (the edible prize) are insufficiently volatile to climb the column under reasonable operating conditions. They self-select out of the feed vessel before the column processes them. The edible fraction walks itself out.

### Stage 4 — IoT Monitoring & Process Control
- Inline sensors throughout: water activity, TPC, free fatty acid %, temperature, pressure, flow rate
- IoT native, continuous data stream
- **Architecture principle:** LLM analytics = advisor only. Process control = deterministic local firmware with hard thresholds. No external write permissions to the line.
- Edge processing on premises. Air gapped controls. Read-only data egress.

## The Closed Loop

The inedible fuel fraction powers the burners that heat the fryers that produce the used oil that feeds the process.

**Zero external energy input at steady state. Zero waste.**

Uncle Rickey's instinct — waste is unconscionable — formalized into an engineering principle.

---

## The Burner System

Used fry oil inedible fraction as fuel presents a viscosity management challenge. Solution: adapt COTS marine bunker fuel burner systems with upstream preheat loop.

- Bunker fuel preheat engineering is mature, well documented, widely serviced
- Our fuel fraction contains essentially no sulfur — burns considerably cleaner than bunker fuel
- Emissions permitting framing: *renewable biosourced fuel with distinctive aromatic profile*
- **Critical design note:** Do NOT fully deodorize the fuel fraction. Residual aromatic compounds are a product feature. See marketing section.
