# Formal Procedure: A Book Concept
*A platform for discerning truth from bullshit*

---

## The Central Idea

Any rigorously expressed discipline — law, science, medicine, mathematics, engineering — has a core series of actions and states that define what it means to do that thing *properly*. These structures, stripped of domain-specific content, are remarkably similar across fields that otherwise have nothing in common. Understanding that shared skeleton is a kind of master key: it lets you evaluate the legitimacy of a process without being an expert in its specific content.

---

## The Platform

Formal procedure is one plank in a broader platform for discerning truth from bullshit. The full platform includes:

- **Formal procedure** — the structured, documented, step-by-step process that makes legitimate inquiry legible and reviewable
- **Logic** — the rules of valid inference connecting each step
- **Evidence standards** — what counts as reliable input to the process
- **Burden of proof** — who must demonstrate what, and to what threshold
- **Good faith** — the foundational commitment to actually pursuing truth, without which all other planks can be gamed

---

## The Platonic Core

The irreducible skeleton of any legitimate process:

1. **A defined starting state** — where you are before you begin is clearly established
2. **A legitimizing authority** — something that sanctions the process as valid
3. **Prescribed transitions** — allowable moves from state to state, with criteria justifying each
4. **Witnessing and documentation** — a mechanism making the process legible and reviewable
5. **A recognized terminal state** — a defined condition that counts as conclusion

This structure appears whether you are running a criminal trial, a clinical study, or a mathematical proof.

---

## Reproducibility and Documentation

Results must be reproducible. For that to be true, they must be documented. Documentation is not bureaucratic formality — it is the mechanism that opens work up to scrutiny from *outside* the immediate guild.

If standards are domain-specific, only insiders can challenge you. If the framework is shared and abstract, someone from an adjacent field can evaluate the structural integrity of what you did without mastering your specific content. This widens the net of valid critics enormously.

---

## Oracles and Verification

Formal authorities — institutions, experts, credentialing bodies — function as useful oracles. They compress verification work: you don't have to personally reproduce every result, you can provisionally trust a reliable authority's endorsement.

But the key word is *provisionally*. The oracle is always in principle verifiable. Authority is on loan from the underlying process, not the other way around. Institutions derive legitimacy from rigorous procedure — not the reverse.

Reproduction count is a slow-motion audit of formal authority. Oracles whose results consistently reproduce accumulate credibility. Those that don't gradually lose the right to be taken at their word, even if they retain institutional trappings.

---

## Logical Meritocracy

The destination of this platform is logical meritocracy: a system where merit is structural, not personal, political, or credentialed. You either did the thing right or you didn't, and the proof is in whether others can follow your steps and arrive at the same place.

This system is self-correcting in a way social hierarchies are not. It doesn't protect incumbents — it exposes them to more scrutiny the more authority they accumulate. A nobody with rigorous work can challenge a somebody with sloppy work, and the shared framework provides the common language to adjudicate that fairly.

---

## The Book

**Audience:** The curious public — intellectually restless people who want to understand how legitimate authority actually works, not just how to navigate it.

**Voice:** Personal, rigorous enough to advise, interesting enough to finish.

**Format:** A tour of disciplines — law, science, medicine, mathematics, engineering — using vivid examples to make an abstract pattern jump out clearly across domains.

**The promise:** A reader who finishes it should be meaningfully better equipped to evaluate a claim, spot procedural theater, and understand why reproducibility matters. They should walk away with a portable framework for recognizing genuine rigor — and its absence — anywhere they encounter it.
