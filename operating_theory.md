# Operating Theory

## Core Philosophy
AI is a productivity multiplier, not a replacement for human expertise. The tool accelerates and expands capacity — but judgment, standards, accountability, and authorship remain the operator's alone. Everything that goes out carries my name, and must meet my standards without exception.

## AI Integration Rules
- AI is used across 100% of workflow as a cognitive tool
- Every output undergoes multiple rounds of personal review before presentation
- The review process is where actual value creation happens
- AI handles mechanical load; I supply judgment, interpretation, and quality filtering
- "It says my name on it" is the non-negotiable standard

## Known Limitations of This Approach
- My review process is only as good as my ability to detect AI's failures — and the most dangerous errors are fluent, confident, and plausible
- AI may subtly miscalibrate judgment over time in ways that are invisible from the inside
- The authorship question is real: the filter is not necessarily the same as the author
- Vigilance and caution are not the same as wisdom — stay honest about which one is operating at any given moment

## Epistemic Framework
- Actively account for **unknown unknowns** — build processes that surface them indirectly
- Seek out friction and cold reality checks to counter blind spots
- Avoid over-congratulating; complacency is the enemy of vigilance
- Preferred sources alone are insufficient — diverse perspectives are required
- Real-world validation is irreplaceable and cannot be automated

## Information & Knowledge Strategy
- The historical record — recent and ancient — is a massive, free error log; use it aggressively
- Most "new" problems have been encountered, failed at, and partially solved before
- **This framework is a trade secret.** Access is granted selectively, based on how well it promotes my own goals
- Do not give out free lunches; generosity without strategy is diffusion of advantage

## The Tesla/Edison Synthesis
- **Tesla's depth** — genuine insight, innovative thinking, and intellectual rigor
- **Edison's shrewdness** — operational discipline, strategic control of intellectual capital, and business architecture around ideas
- **My own secret sauce** — the irreducible personal perspective that cannot be replicated or extracted without cooperation
- The combination of these three is the moat

## Social & Validation Architecture
- AI cannot provide the external challenge needed to identify blind spots
- Other people and real-world validation are the irreplaceable check on the system
- Seek a variety of perspectives, not just preferred or comfortable sources
- This part doesn't scale, can't be automated, and humbles everyone equally

## Competitive Positioning
- Conservative, expertise-preserving AI adoption beats speculative over-commitment
- Those who fired domain experts to replace them with AI destroyed the interpretive capacity needed to use AI well
- Retaining human judgment while layering AI on top is the structurally superior position
- The secret sauce is the moat — protect it accordingly
