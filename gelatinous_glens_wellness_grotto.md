# Gelatinous Glen's Wellness Grotto
*"You'll Come Out Different Than You Went In"*

*Established sometime ago. All cubes are happy and healthy and your safety is our priority. Thud has never lost anyone.*
*Thud would like you to know he takes this very seriously.*

---

## Ownership & Management

**Glen** is the owner, founder, and nominal head of operations. He is older, disheveled, and genuinely passionate about ooze biology in a way that makes clients slightly uncomfortable. He refers to each cube by name. He tears up occasionally when describing how far Blobsworth has come as an employee. He has a framed portrait of his first cube on the office wall. The portrait is a painting of an empty room.

Glen is nominally in charge. Everyone knows what "nominally" means here.

**Grix** is the goblin matron and operational backbone of the entire establishment — both sides of it. Four foot two of absolute professional authority. Has worked for Glen longer than most of the cubes have been alive. Knows where everything is, what everything costs, and exactly how much she doesn't want to hear about it. Runs the front of house and the back entrance with identical efficiency and zero moral commentary. This is a job. She is good at her job.

### The Agreement

Glen and Grix have a standing arrangement, documented nowhere and understood completely, covering the orderly dissolution of operations should circumstances ever require sudden departure. The terms were negotiated once, early on, over tea. Glen asked one question. Grix answered it. He has not asked anything since.

The arrangement covers: asset liquidation, cube relocation to pre-arranged sanctuary placements, destruction of Grix's ledger (which she handles personally), and a severance structure that Glen has privately concluded is quite fair. The cubes are always provided for. This was Glen's only non-negotiable condition. Grix accepted it without comment.

Neither of them expects to use the arrangement. Both of them are glad it exists.

## The Staff

**Marta** — Front desk receptionist. Unnervingly cheerful. Slides the liability waiver across the desk without breaking eye contact or her smile. The waiver is extremely long. She says "it's mostly standard" about every page.

**Thud** — Winch operator and client safety anchor. A massive, quiet half-orc of very few words. Incredibly gentle. Has never lost a client. Very proud of this. Mentions it unprompted. Every time. Thud has conditions about which operations he participates in. Glen respects this. Thud's record remains perfect because Thud defined the parameters of his record carefully.

**Sister Velantine** — Attendant cleric. Technically still ordained, though she won't say by whom anymore. Hums while casting. The hymns are slightly wrong in ways clients cannot quite identify. Her healing magic works perfectly, which she seems to find mildly surprising each time.

**Pip** — Apprentice potion mixer. Enthusiastic, undertrained, labels everything in handwriting that is difficult to read. There have been incidents. Nothing fatal. Pip considers this a success metric. Pip and Kevin have a bond. This is not reassuring.

## The Cubes

**Blobsworth** — Senior cube and Glen's personal favorite. Given preferential treatment in subtle ways. The other cubes know. Blobsworth knows they know.

**Muriel** — The gentle one. Recommended for first-timers and nervous clients. Somehow communicates reassurance despite having no face, no discernible personality, and no biological mechanism for empathy. Glen insists she is nurturing. He is probably right.

**Kevin** — Newer hire. Still learning. Occasionally miscalibrates acid output in ways that are harmless but noticeable. Shares a bond with Pip. See above note regarding reassurance.

**The One In The Back** — Nobody on staff refers to it by name. It does not appear on the service menu. Glen describes it as semi-retired. It is very large. It is very still. Clients who ask about it are gently redirected by Grix, who does this without looking up from her ledger.

## Safety Infrastructure

The treatment environment has been engineered to a standard exceeding most licensed medical facilities in the region.

- **Chainmail bikini harness** on a chain — maximum cube contact surface, zero dissolution risk, attached to winch
- **Chain and winch** operated by Thud — mechanical extraction, primary emergency response
- **Glass mask with flexible steel breathing tube** — airway completely isolated from cube medium, full visibility maintained
- **Dominated cube** — cooperative rather than predatory; modulates own acid output voluntarily
- **Continuous freshwater flush** — real-time pH dilution and management throughout treatment
- **Optional potion-infused flush** — healing, antitoxin, or vitality compounds delivered transdermally across full body surface
- **Attendant spellcaster** (Sister Velantine) — active monitoring, intervention, and casting capability throughout session
- **Dissolution potion on standby** — emergency cube termination, stored at each treatment station like a fire extinguisher
- **Regeneration spell available** — significant damage recoverable even in worst-case scenarios

*The cube is statistically the least dangerous element in the building. The winch operator is more likely to cause an injury.*

## Treatment Menu

### Front of House

**The Muriel Classic**
Introductory soak. Mild enzymatic exfoliation. Freshwater flush. Complimentary calming tea served through the snorkel tube by Pip, who has definitely not added anything extra.

**The Blobsworth Prestige**
Full treatment. Potion-infused flush. Sister Velantine on attendant casting duty. Chromotherapy ambient lighting. Thud personally supervising the winch throughout.

**The Kevin Experience**
Discounted rate. Glen is apologetic in advance. Additional waiver required. Thud stays very close to the winch.

**The Restoration Package**
Regeneration spell. Antitoxin flush. Greater Restoration. Full extraction followed by post-soak wrap in warm towels that smell faintly of something herbal and unidentifiable. Clients report feeling twenty years younger. Clients do not ask why Glen has that capability.

### Back Entrance
*Obscure alley. Knock twice. Have payment. Don't describe it.*
*Managed exclusively by Grix.*

**Standard** — One cube. Standard duration. No questions asked or answered.

**Thorough** — Blobsworth personally. Glen remains upstairs. Grix handles everything.

**The One In The Back** — Price negotiated in person. Cash equivalent only. Grix looks at the client for a long moment before confirming. Three clients have requested the front-of-house equivalent of this service out of curiosity. All three came out looking profoundly calm and subtly changed in ways nobody could specify. All three left five-star reviews.

*Paperwork for the back entrance is surprisingly thorough. Grix insists on this. It protects everyone. She does not specify who "everyone" includes.*
